About A-level Chemistry A²

A-level Chemistry A² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE A level Chemistry A.

The notes follow the structure of the OCR Advanced GCE in Chemistry A (H432) specification, covering Modules 2–6. Module 1 practical skills are covered within the topics. Modules 2–4 are also in the AS course (H032).

A-level Chemistry A² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
