About Biology²

Biology² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE Biology.

The notes follow the structure of the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Biology (4BI1) specification, covering all five topics including Paper 2-only content. A final section covers the extra content in the Pearson Edexcel GCSE Biology (1BI0) course.

Biology² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
