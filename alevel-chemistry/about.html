About A-level Chemistry²

A-level Chemistry² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE A level Chemistry.

The notes follow the structure of the Pearson Edexcel Level 3 Advanced GCE in Chemistry (9CH0) specification, covering all 19 topics. Topics 1–10 are also in the AS course (8CH0).

A-level Chemistry² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
