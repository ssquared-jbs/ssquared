Privacy Policy

A-level Chemistry² respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any information collected while using the website.

Information We May Collect

A-level Chemistry² may collect limited information such as anonymous usage statistics and page visits to help improve the platform and user experience.

Your Progress

If you don't sign in, your progress, scores, streak and notes are saved only in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Accounts (optional)

You can choose to sign in with Google, Microsoft or an email address so your progress follows you between devices. Sign-in and storage are provided by Google Firebase. Your account holds your name, email address, profile picture (if your Google or Microsoft account has one), and your progress, XP, streak and notes on Ssquared HQ. We never see your password.

We use this information only to run your account. We don't sell it or use it for advertising. You can delete your account and everything stored in it at any time: open the account menu and choose "Delete my account". Signing out removes your account's progress from that device.

Cookies

This website may use cookies to improve functionality and user experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device by your web browser.

Advertising

A-level Chemistry² may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense. These services may use cookies to display personalised advertisements based on your browsing activity. Google may use advertising cookies to serve ads to users based on visits to this and other websites.

Third-Party Services

Some third-party tools or services used on A-level Chemistry² may collect anonymous data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally as the platform develops and new features are added.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please get in touch through the contact page.
