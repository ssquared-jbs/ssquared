About English Language²

English Language² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE English Language.

The notes follow the Pearson Edexcel Level 1/Level 2 GCSE (9–1) in English Language (1EN0): Paper 1 (Fiction and Imaginative Writing), Paper 2 (Non-fiction and Transactional Writing) and the Spoken Language endorsement. All example extracts are either original or from out-of-copyright 19th-century texts.

English Language² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
