About Economics²

Economics² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE Economics.

Pearson Edexcel does not offer a GCSE in Economics, so these notes follow AQA GCSE Economics (8136): Paper 1 (How markets work) and Paper 2 (How the economy works).

Economics² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
