About A-level Psychology²

A-level Psychology² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE A level Psychology.

The notes follow the Pearson Edexcel Level 3 Advanced GCE in Psychology (9PS0): the four foundation topics, clinical psychology, the criminological and child psychology options, and psychological skills.

A-level Psychology² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
