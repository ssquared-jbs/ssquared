About Computer Science²

Computer Science² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE Computer Science.

The notes follow the Pearson Edexcel Level 1/Level 2 GCSE (9–1) in Computer Science (1CP2): Paper 1 (Principles of Computer Science, topics 1–5) and Paper 2 (Application of Computational Thinking, topic 6, an on-screen Python exam). Code examples use Python 3.

Computer Science² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
