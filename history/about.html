About History²

History² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE History.

The notes follow the Pearson Edexcel Level 1/Level 2 GCSE (9–1) in History (1HI0), covering the most popular options: Medicine in Britain c1250–present with the Western Front 1914–18 (Paper 1), Superpower relations and the Cold War 1941–91 and Early Elizabethan England 1558–88 (Paper 2), and Weimar and Nazi Germany 1918–39 (Paper 3).

History² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
