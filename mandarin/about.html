About Mandarin²

Mandarin² is an interactive GCSE Mandarin revision platform designed to help students revise vocabulary, grammar, reading, writing and exam skills in a more engaging and effective way.

The platform combines structured learning pages, quizzes, flashcards and interactive games to help students build confidence in Mandarin both inside and outside the classroom.

Why Mandarin² Was Created

Mandarin resources can often feel limited compared to subjects such as Maths or Science. Mandarin² was created to provide students with a modern revision platform specifically focused on GCSE Mandarin learning.

The aim is to make revision more interactive, accessible and enjoyable while still helping students achieve strong exam results.

Features

  • Interactive Learn sections
  • Topic quizzes
  • Flashcards
  • 3+2 revision game mode
  • Exam-focused vocabulary practice
  • Progressive topic structure

Future Development

Mandarin² is currently in active development, with more topics, games, exam practice and student features planned in future updates.
