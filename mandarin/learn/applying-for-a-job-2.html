Skills and Strengths

Learn how to describe your abilities, personality and why you are suitable for a job.

1. Positive qualities

认真

rèn zhēn

hardworking / serious

友好

yǒu hǎo

friendly

努力

nǔ lì

hardworking

可靠

kě kào

reliable

我是一个很认真的人。

I am a very hardworking person.

我的同事觉得我很可靠。

My colleagues think I am very reliable.

Quick Check

2. Skills and abilities

技能

jì néng

skill

经验

jīng yàn

experience

沟通能力

gōu tōng néng lì

communication skills

团队合作

tuán duì hé zuò

teamwork

我有很好的沟通能力。

I have very good communication skills.

我喜欢团队合作。

I enjoy teamwork.

Check

3. Languages and study

中文

zhōng wén

Chinese language

英文

yīng wén

English language

学习

xué xí

to study

提高

tí gāo

to improve

我会说中文和英文。

I can speak Chinese and English.

我想提高我的中文。

I want to improve my Chinese.

4. Reasons for applying

因为

yīn wèi

because

所以

suǒ yǐ

so / therefore

机会

jī huì

opportunity

将来

jiāng lái

future

因为我喜欢这个公司，所以我想申请这个工作。

Because I like this company, I want to apply for this job.

这个机会对我的将来很有帮助。

This opportunity is very helpful for my future.

Final Check

Complete

我是一个认真的人，也很可靠。我有很好的沟通能力，而且我喜欢团队合作。因为这个机会对我的将来有帮助，所以我想申请这个工作。

I am a hardworking and reliable person. I have very good communication skills and I enjoy teamwork. Because this opportunity is helpful for my future, I want to apply for this job.
