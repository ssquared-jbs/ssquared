Job Applications

Learn how to talk about applying for jobs, CVs, forms and applications.

1. Applying for jobs

申请工作

shēn qǐng gōng zuò

to apply for a job

申请表

shēn qǐng biǎo

application form

工作

gōng zuò

job / work

公司

gōng sī

company

我想申请这个工作。

I want to apply for this job.

我已经填写了申请表。

I have already filled in the application form.

Quick Check

2. CVs and documents

简历

jiǎn lì

CV / résumé

文件

wén jiàn

document

发送

fā sòng

to send

电子邮件

diàn zǐ yóu jiàn

email

我发送了我的简历。

I sent my CV.

我用电子邮件发送文件。

I sent the documents by email.

Check

3. Interviews

面试

miàn shì

interview

问题

wèn tí

question

回答

huí dá

to answer

经理

jīng lǐ

manager

经理问了我很多问题。

The manager asked me many questions.

我认真回答了问题。

I answered the questions seriously.

4. Results and future

成功

chéng gōng

success / successful

机会

jī huì

opportunity

将来

jiāng lái

future

职业

zhí yè

career

这个工作机会很好。

This job opportunity is very good.

我希望将来有成功的职业。

I hope to have a successful career in the future.

Final Check

Complete

我想申请这个工作，所以我填写了申请表，也发送了我的简历。面试的时候，经理问了我很多问题。

I wanted to apply for this job, so I filled in the application form and also sent my CV. During the interview, the manager asked me many questions.
