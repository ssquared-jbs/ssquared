Booking and Preparation

Learn vocabulary for tickets, hotels, passports, visas and travel preparation.

1. Booking transport

订票

dìng piào

to book tickets

机票

jī piào

plane ticket

火车票

huǒ chē piào

train ticket

出发

chū fā

to depart

我已经订了机票。

I have already booked the plane tickets.

我们明天出发。

We are departing tomorrow.

Quick Check

2. Hotels and rooms

宾馆

bīn guǎn

hotel

房间

fáng jiān

room

双人房

shuāng rén fáng

double room

住

zhù

to stay / live

我们订了一个双人房。

We booked a double room.

我想住在市中心的宾馆。

I want to stay in a hotel in the city centre.

Check

3. Travel documents

护照

hù zhào

passport

签证

qiān zhèng

visa

身份证

shēn fèn zhèng

ID card

准备好

zhǔn bèi hǎo

to prepare fully

我的护照已经准备好了。

My passport is already prepared.

去中国需要签证。

You need a visa to go to China.

4. Packing and preparation

行李

xíng li

luggage

收拾

shōu shi

to pack / tidy

地图

dì tú

map

安排

ān pái

to arrange

我正在收拾行李。

I am packing my luggage.

我已经安排好了旅行。

I have already arranged the trip.

Final Check

Complete

我已经订了机票和宾馆。我准备好了护照和签证，现在正在收拾行李。我们明天出发。

I have already booked the plane tickets and hotel. I have prepared my passport and visa, and now I am packing my luggage. We are departing tomorrow.
