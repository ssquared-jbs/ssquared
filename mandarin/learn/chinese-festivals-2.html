Final Check

Complete

春节的时候，人们挂灯笼，也会收红包。很多家人和亲戚一起聚会。我觉得春节的气氛特别，也很开心。

During Spring Festival, people hang lanterns and also receive red envelopes. Many family members and relatives gather together. I think the atmosphere of Spring Festival is special and very happy.
