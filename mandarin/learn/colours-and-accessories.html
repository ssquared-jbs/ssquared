Colours and Accessories

Learn colours, accessories and useful clothing descriptions.

1. Colours

颜色

yán sè

colour

红色

hóng sè

red

白色

bái sè

white

黄色

huáng sè

yellow

蓝色

lán sè

blue

橙色

chéng sè

orange

黑色

hēi sè

black

绿色

lǜ sè

green

我喜欢蓝色和黑色。

I like blue and black.

Quick Check

2. Accessories

带

dài

to bring / carry / wear

领带

lǐng dài

tie

帽子

mào zi

hat / cap

手表

shǒu biǎo

watch

眼镜

yǎn jìng

glasses

我戴眼镜，也戴手表。

I wear glasses and also wear a watch.

他戴黑色的帽子。

He wears a black hat.

Check

3. Extra clothing words

长袖

cháng xiù

long sleeve

短袖

duǎn xiù

short sleeve

国旗

guó qí

flag

我喜欢穿白色的短袖。

I like wearing a white short-sleeved shirt.

国旗是红色、白色和蓝色的。

The flag is red, white and blue.

Final Check

Complete

我喜欢蓝色和黑色。我戴眼镜，也戴手表。我喜欢穿白色的短袖。

I like blue and black. I wear glasses and also wear a watch. I like wearing a white short-sleeved shirt.
