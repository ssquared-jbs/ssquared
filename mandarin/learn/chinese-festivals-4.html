Complex Festival Opinions

Use reasons, contrasts and stronger structures to give exam-style opinions about Chinese festivals.

1. Giving festival opinions

我认为……

wǒ rèn wéi...

I believe...

我觉得……

wǒ jué de...

I think...

传统节日

chuán tǒng jié rì

traditional festival

文化

wén huà

culture

我认为传统节日很重要。

I believe traditional festivals are very important.

我觉得春节很有意思。

I think Spring Festival is very interesting.

Quick Check

2. Giving reasons

因为

yīn wèi

because

所以

suǒ yǐ

so / therefore

团圆

tuán yuán

family reunion

了解文化

liǎo jiě wén huà

to understand culture

因为春节可以让家人团圆，所以我很喜欢春节。

Because Spring Festival allows families to reunite, I really like Spring Festival.

传统节日可以帮助我们了解文化。

Traditional festivals can help us understand culture.

Check

3. Using contrast

虽然

suī rán

although

但是

dàn shì

but

很忙

hěn máng

very busy

很累

hěn lèi

very tiring

虽然春节很忙，但是非常热闹。

Although Spring Festival is very busy, it is extremely lively.

虽然准备年夜饭很累，但是大家都很开心。

Although preparing New Year’s Eve dinner is tiring, everyone is happy.

4. Strong festival structures

不仅……而且……

bù jǐn... ér qiě...

not only... but also...

最重要的是

zuì zhòng yào de shì

most importantly

让我难忘

ràng wǒ nán wàng

made it unforgettable for me

值得庆祝

zhí de qìng zhù

worth celebrating

春节不仅有意思，而且对中国文化很重要。

Spring Festival is not only interesting, but also important for Chinese culture.

最重要的是，这个节日值得庆祝。

Most importantly, this festival is worth celebrating.

Final Check

Complete

我认为传统节日很重要，因为它们可以帮助我们了解文化。虽然春节很忙，但是非常热闹。春节不仅有意思，而且让家人团圆。最重要的是，这个节日值得庆祝。

I believe traditional festivals are important because they can help us understand culture. Although Spring Festival is very busy, it is extremely lively. Spring Festival is not only interesting, but also lets families reunite. Most importantly, this festival is worth celebrating.
