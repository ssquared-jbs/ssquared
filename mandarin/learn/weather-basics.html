Weather Basics

Learn key weather vocabulary and how to describe the weather.

1. Common Weather

天气

tiān qì

weather

晴天

qíng tiān

sunny day

阴天

yīn tiān

cloudy

多云

duō yún

partly cloudy

有雾

yǒu wù

foggy

今天天气很好，是晴天。

The weather today is good, it is sunny.

Quick Check

2. Rain, wind and snow

下雨

xià yǔ

to rain

下雪

xià xuě

to snow

刮风

guā fēng

windy

风

fēng

wind

今天下雨，也刮风。

Today it is raining and windy.

Check

3. Temperature

热

rè

hot

冷

lěng

cold

气温

qì wēn

temperature

度

dù

degree

今天很热，气温三十度。

Today is very hot, the temperature is 30 degrees.

4. Forecast and cities

天气预报

tiān qì yù bào

weather forecast

上海

Shàng hǎi

Shanghai

西安

Xī ān

Xi’an

南京

Nán jīng

Nanjing

天气预报说明天上海很热。

The weather forecast says Shanghai will be hot tomorrow.

Final Check

Complete

今天天气很好，是晴天，但是有一点风。气温二十五度。

Today the weather is good, it is sunny but a little windy. The temperature is 25 degrees.
