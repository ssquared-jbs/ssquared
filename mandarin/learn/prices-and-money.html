Prices and Money

Learn how to talk about prices, measurements and shopping quantities.

1. Calligraphy items

毛笔

máo bǐ

calligraphy brush

墨水

mò shuǐ

ink

宣纸

xuān zhǐ

rice paper

砚台

yàn tái

ink stone

我买毛笔和墨水。

I buy a calligraphy brush and ink.

Quick Check

2. Basic items

雨伞

yǔ sǎn

umbrella

铅笔

qiān bǐ

pencil

我买铅笔和雨伞。

I buy pencils and an umbrella.

Check

3. Prices and quantity

便宜

pián yi

cheap

块

kuài

yuan (money)

斤

jīn

500g

公斤

gōng jīn

kilogram

一共

yí gòng

in total

这个很便宜，只要十块钱。

This is very cheap, it only costs 10 yuan.

4. Locations and distance

市中心

shì zhōng xīn

city centre

以外

yǐ wài

outside

公里

gōng lǐ

kilometre

商店在市中心以外五公里。

The shop is 5 kilometres outside the city centre.

Final Check

Complete

我买铅笔和雨伞。一共二十块钱，很便宜。

I buy pencils and an umbrella. In total it is 20 yuan, very cheap.
