3. Renting and suitability

租

zū

to rent

租房

zū fáng

rent a flat / rent a house

两房一厅

liǎng fáng yì tīng

two bedrooms, one living room

适合

suitable

方便

fāng biàn

convenient

安静

quiet

我想租房。两房一厅很适合我家。

I want to rent a flat. Two bedrooms and one living room is very suitable for my family.

这里很安静，也很方便。

It is very quiet here and also very convenient.
