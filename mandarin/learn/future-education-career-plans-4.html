Complex Future Plans

Use reasons, contrasts and advanced structures to explain your future education and career plans.

1. Explaining plans clearly

打算

dǎ suàn

to plan to

希望

xī wàng

to hope

决定

jué dìng

to decide

目标

mù biāo

goal

我打算上大学。

I plan to go to university.

我的目标是将来当医生。

My goal is to become a doctor in the future.

Quick Check

2. Giving reasons

因为

yīn wèi

because

所以

suǒ yǐ

so / therefore

感兴趣

gǎn xìng qù

interested in

有帮助

yǒu bāng zhù

helpful

因为我对科学感兴趣，所以我想学医学。

Because I am interested in science, I want to study medicine.

大学对我的将来很有帮助。

University is very helpful for my future.

Check

3. Using contrast

虽然

suī rán

although

但是

dàn shì

but

困难

kùn nan

difficult

压力

yā lì

pressure / stress

虽然学习很困难，但是我会努力。

Although studying is difficult, I will work hard.

虽然考试压力很大，但是我不会放弃。

Although exam pressure is high, I will not give up.

4. Strong future structures

不仅……而且……

bù jǐn... ér qiě...

not only... but also...

如果有机会

rú guǒ yǒu jī huì

if I have the chance

实现梦想

shí xiàn mèng xiǎng

to achieve a dream

最重要的是

zuì zhòng yào de shì

most importantly

大学不仅重要，而且会给我很多机会。

University is not only important, but will also give me many opportunities.

最重要的是，我想实现我的梦想。

Most importantly, I want to achieve my dream.

Final Check

Complete

我打算上大学，因为我对科学很感兴趣。虽然学习很困难，但是我会努力。大学不仅重要，而且会给我很多机会。最重要的是，我想实现我的梦想。

I plan to go to university because I am very interested in science. Although studying is difficult, I will work hard. University is not only important, but will also give me many opportunities. Most importantly, I want to achieve my dream.
