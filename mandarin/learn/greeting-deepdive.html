Building Conversations

Now we go beyond greetings and start forming real conversations.

1. Asking questions

问

wèn

to ask

什么

shénme

what

吗

ma

question particle

你好吗？

Nǐ hǎo ma?

How are you?

Quick check

2. Talking about feelings

很

hěn

very

高兴

gāoxìng

happy

认识

rènshi

to meet/know

很高兴认识你。

Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.

Nice to meet you.

Check

3. Names and identity

姓

surname

名字

name

叫

to be called

Final check

Complete

You can now build basic conversations.

你好！你叫什么名字？我叫杰瑞，很高兴认识你。我住在北京。
