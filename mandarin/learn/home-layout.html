Home Layout

Learn how to describe rooms, location, size and where things are in a house.

1. Buildings and rooms

层

céng

floor / level

楼

lóu

building

单元

dān yuán

unit

间

jiān

measure word for rooms

平方米

píng fāng mǐ

square metre

我家有三间房，左右一百平方米。

My home has three rooms and is about 100 square metres.

2. Position words

right side

左边

zuǒ bian

left side

外边

wài bian

outside

前边

qián bian

front

里边

lǐ bian

inside

后面

hòu mian

behind

厨房在客厅的右边。

The kitchen is on the right of the living room.

3. More location words

上边

shàng bian

above

下边

xià bian

below

中间

zhōng jiān

middle / centre

旁边

páng biān

beside

左右

zuǒ yòu

approximately

电视机在桌子的上边。

The TV is above the table.

书房在卧室旁边。

The study room is beside the bedroom.

4. Objects and descriptions

电视机

diàn shì jī

TV

收音机

shōu yīn jī

radio

干净

gān jìng

clean

坏

huài

broken

乱

luàn

messy

只有

zhǐ yǒu

only

我的房间很干净，但是电视机坏了。

My room is clean, but the TV is broken.

5. Extra phrases

进

jìn

to enter

门

mén

door

谁的

shuí de

whose

三室一厅

sān shì yì tīng

three bedrooms and one living room

狗窝

gǒu wō

kennel

这是谁的房间？

Whose room is this?

狗窝在花园里边。

The kennel is inside the garden.

Final Check

Complete

我家有三室一厅，左右一百平方米。客厅很干净，电视机在桌子上边。

My home has three bedrooms and one living room, about 100 square metres. The living room is clean, and the TV is above the table.
