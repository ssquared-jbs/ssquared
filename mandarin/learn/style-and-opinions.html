Style and Opinions

Learn how to describe how clothes look and give opinions.

1. Describing clothes

咖啡色

kā fēi sè

brown

肥

féi

loose / big

什么样的

shén me yàng de

what it looks like

好看

hǎo kàn

good looking

酷

kù

cool

这件衣服很好看，也很酷。

This piece of clothing looks good and is also cool.

Quick Check

2. Fashion opinions

老土

lǎo tǔ

old-fashioned

时髦

shí máo

trendy / fashionable

我觉得这件衣服很时髦。

I think this piece of clothing is very fashionable.

那件衣服有一点老土。

That piece of clothing is a bit old-fashioned.

Final Check

Complete

我觉得这件衣服很好看，也很时髦，但是有一点肥。

I think this piece of clothing looks good and is fashionable, but it is a bit loose.
