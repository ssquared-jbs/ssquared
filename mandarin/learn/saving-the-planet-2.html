Helping the Environment

Learn how to talk about recycling, saving energy and protecting nature.

1. Recycling

回收

huí shōu

to recycle

纸

zhǐ

paper

瓶子

píng zi

bottle

减少垃圾

jiǎn shǎo lā jī

to reduce rubbish

我们应该回收纸和瓶子。

We should recycle paper and bottles.

回收可以减少垃圾。

Recycling can reduce rubbish.

Quick Check

2. Saving energy and water

节约能源

jié yuē néng yuán

to save energy

节约用水

jié yuē yòng shuǐ

to save water

关灯

guān dēng

to turn off lights

浪费

làng fèi

to waste

离开房间的时候，我会关灯。

When leaving a room, I turn off the lights.

我们不应该浪费水。

We should not waste water.

Check

3. Transport and travel

骑自行车

qí zì xíng chē

to ride a bike

坐公交车

zuò gōng jiāo chē

to take the bus

步行

bù xíng

to walk

汽车

qì chē

car

为了保护环境，我常常步行。

To protect the environment, I often walk.

少开汽车对环境更好。

Using cars less is better for the environment.

4. Protecting nature

种树

zhòng shù

to plant trees

保护动物

bǎo hù dòng wù

to protect animals

环保

huán bǎo

environmental protection

责任

zé rèn

responsibility

每个人都有保护环境的责任。

Everyone has a responsibility to protect the environment.

种树可以帮助地球。

Planting trees can help the planet.

Final Check

Complete

我们应该回收纸和瓶子，也不应该浪费水。为了保护环境，我常常步行，很少开汽车。我觉得每个人都有保护环境的责任，因为环保对地球很重要。

We should recycle paper and bottles, and we should not waste water. To protect the environment, I often walk and rarely use cars. I think everyone has a responsibility to protect the environment because environmental protection is important for the planet.
