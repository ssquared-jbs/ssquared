Ordering Food

Learn how to order food, ask for help, describe taste and speak politely in restaurants.

1. Ordering in a restaurant

服务员

fúwùyuán

waiter / waitress

点菜

diǎn cài

order food

要

yào

want / would like

还是

háishi

or

服务员，我要点菜。

Waiter, I would like to order food.

你要茶还是水？

Do you want tea or water?

Quick Check

2. Asking for help

加

jiā

add

杯子

bēizi

cup / glass

帮

bāng

help

拿

ná

take / bring

请你来

qǐng nǐ lái

please come

请你来帮我拿杯子。

Please come and help me bring a cup.

请加一杯水。

Please add one cup of water.

Check

3. Describing food and people

友好

yǒuhǎo

friendly

尝尝

chángchang

have a taste

口渴

kǒukě

thirsty

新鲜

xīnxiān

fresh

味道

wèidao

taste / flavour

这个服务员很友好。

This waiter is very friendly.

这个水果很新鲜，味道很好。

This fruit is fresh and the taste is good.

4. Linking ideas

与

yǔ

and / with

时评

shípíng

current affairs commentary

就是

jiùshì

exactly / that is

我喜欢茶与点心。

I like tea and dim sum.

这就是我喜欢的味道。

This is exactly the taste I like.

Final Check

Complete

服务员，我要点菜。请你来帮我拿杯子。我口渴，想喝水。这个菜很新鲜，味道很好。

Waiter, I would like to order food. Please come and help me bring a cup. I am thirsty and want to drink water. This dish is fresh and tastes good.
