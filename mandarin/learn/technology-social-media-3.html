Advantages and Disadvantages

Learn how to discuss the benefits and problems of technology and social media.

1. Advantages

优点

yōu diǎn

advantage

方便

fāng biàn

convenient

有用

yǒu yòng

useful

节省时间

jié shěng shí jiān

to save time

互联网的优点是很方便。

The advantage of the internet is that it is very convenient.

手机可以节省时间。

Mobile phones can save time.

Quick Check

2. Disadvantages

缺点

quē diǎn

disadvantage

危险

wēi xiǎn

dangerous

浪费时间

làng fèi shí jiān

to waste time

上瘾

shàng yǐn

addicted

社交媒体的缺点是会浪费时间。

The disadvantage of social media is that it can waste time.

有些人对手机上瘾。

Some people are addicted to their phones.

Check

3. Online safety

安全

ān quán

safe / safety

隐私

yǐn sī

privacy

保护

bǎo hù

to protect

个人信息

gè rén xìn xī

personal information

上网的时候，安全很重要。

When going online, safety is very important.

我们应该保护个人信息。

We should protect personal information.

4. Balanced opinions

虽然

suī rán

although

但是

dàn shì

but

好处

hǎo chù

benefit

坏处

huài chù

bad point

虽然手机很方便，但是也有坏处。

Although phones are convenient, they also have bad points.

社交媒体有好处，也有坏处。

Social media has benefits and bad points.

Final Check

Complete

我觉得互联网很方便，也很有用。虽然社交媒体有很多好处，但是它也会浪费时间。上网的时候，我们应该保护个人信息。

I think the internet is very convenient and useful. Although social media has many benefits, it can also waste time. When going online, we should protect personal information.
