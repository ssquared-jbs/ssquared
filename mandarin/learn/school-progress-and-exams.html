Progress and Exams

Learn how to talk about progress, exams, results and school opinions.

1. Progress and opinions

进步

jìn bù

to improve

愉快

yú kuài

happy

困难

kùn nan

difficult

差

chà

poor / bad

还可以

hái kě yǐ

not too bad / acceptable

有趣

yǒu qù

interesting

我的汉语有进步，所以我很愉快。

My Chinese has improved, so I am very happy.

物理很困难，但是还可以。

Physics is difficult, but it is still acceptable.

Quick Check

2. Exams and results

事情

shì qing

things / matters

点钟

diǎn zhōng

o’clock

假期

jià qī

holiday / vacation

考试

kǎo shì

examination

成绩

chéng jì

result / score

八点钟我有考试。

At eight o’clock I have an exam.

我的成绩不错。

My results are not bad.

Check

3. School events and language

家长会

jiā zhǎng huì

parents’ meeting

语言

yǔ yán

language

贵

guì

expensive

明天学校有家长会。

Tomorrow the school has a parents’ meeting.

学习语言很有趣，但是有时候很贵。

Learning languages is interesting, but sometimes expensive.

Final Check

Complete

我的汉语有进步，成绩也不错。考试很困难，但是学习语言很有趣。

My Chinese has improved, and my results are also not bad. Exams are difficult, but learning languages is interesting.
