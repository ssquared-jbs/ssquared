Complex Travel Plans

Use reasons, future structures and contrast phrases to make your travel planning answers stronger.

1. Explaining future plans

打算

dǎ suàn

to plan to

会

huì

will

希望

xī wàng

to hope

如果有机会

rú guǒ yǒu jī huì

if I have the chance

如果有机会，我会去中国旅行。

If I have the chance, I will travel to China.

我希望明年可以去北京。

I hope I can go to Beijing next year.

Quick Check

2. Giving reasons

因为

yīn wèi

because

所以

suǒ yǐ

so / therefore

想了解

xiǎng liǎo jiě

want to understand / learn about

文化

wén huà

culture

因为我想了解中国文化，所以我打算去北京。

Because I want to learn about Chinese culture, I plan to go to Beijing.

我想去上海，因为那里很现代化。

I want to go to Shanghai because it is very modern there.

Check

3. Using contrast

虽然

suī rán

although

但是

dàn shì

but

可是

kě shì

however

贵

guì

expensive

虽然机票很贵，但是我觉得很值得。

Although the plane ticket is expensive, I think it is worth it.

虽然旅行很麻烦，可是我很期待。

Although travelling is troublesome, I am looking forward to it.

4. Strong planning sentences

不仅……而且……

bù jǐn... ér qiě...

not only... but also...

提前

tí qián

in advance

值得

zhí de

worth it

期待

qī dài

to look forward to

我不仅订了机票，而且提前安排好了宾馆。

I not only booked plane tickets, but also arranged the hotel in advance.

我很期待这次旅行。

I am really looking forward to this trip.

Final Check

Complete

如果有机会，我会去中国旅行。因为我想了解中国文化，所以我打算去北京。虽然机票很贵，但是我觉得很值得。我不仅订了机票，而且提前安排好了宾馆。

If I have the chance, I will travel to China. Because I want to learn about Chinese culture, I plan to go to Beijing. Although the plane ticket is expensive, I think it is worth it. I not only booked plane tickets, but also arranged the hotel in advance.
