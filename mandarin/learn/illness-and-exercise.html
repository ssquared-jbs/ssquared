Illness and Exercise

Learn how to talk about illness, doctors and staying healthy.

1. Illness

病

bìng

illness

发烧

fā shāo

fever

感冒

gǎn mào

cold / flu

疼

téng

pain

我感冒了，还有点儿发烧。

I have a cold and also a bit of a fever.

Quick Check

2. Doctors and hospitals

看病

kàn bìng

see a doctor

医生

yī shēng

doctor

医院

yī yuàn

hospital

住院

zhù yuàn

hospitalised

药房

yào fáng

pharmacy

我去医院看病。

I go to the hospital to see a doctor.

Check

3. Exercise and health

散步

sàn bù

walk

锻炼身体

duàn liàn shēn tǐ

exercise

开心

kāi xīn

happy

聊天

liáo tiān

chat

我喜欢散步和锻炼身体。

I like walking and exercising.

我和朋友聊天，很开心。

I chat with friends and feel happy.

Final Check

Complete

我感冒了，所以去医院看病。平时我喜欢散步和锻炼身体，这对身体很好。

I had a cold, so I went to the hospital. Normally I like walking and exercising, which is good for my health.
