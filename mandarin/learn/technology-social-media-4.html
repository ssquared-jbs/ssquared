Complex Technology Opinions

Use reasons, contrasts and stronger structures to give exam-style opinions about technology.

1. Giving opinions

我认为……

wǒ rèn wéi...

I believe...

我觉得……

wǒ jué de...

I think...

对我来说

duì wǒ lái shuō

for me

重要

zhòng yào

important

我认为互联网很重要。

I believe the internet is very important.

对我来说，手机很有用。

For me, mobile phones are very useful.

Quick Check

2. Explaining reasons

因为

yīn wèi

because

所以

suǒ yǐ

so / therefore

帮助学习

bāng zhù xué xí

to help studying

联系朋友

lián xì péng you

to contact friends

因为互联网帮助学习，所以我每天都用它。

Because the internet helps studying, I use it every day.

手机可以联系朋友，所以很方便。

Phones can contact friends, so they are very convenient.

Check

3. Using contrast

虽然

suī rán

although

但是

dàn shì

but

分心

fēn xīn

to be distracted

影响健康

yǐng xiǎng jiàn kāng

to affect health

虽然手机很方便，但是会让人分心。

Although phones are convenient, they can distract people.

虽然社交媒体有用，但是用太久会影响健康。

Although social media is useful, using it for too long can affect health.

4. Strong exam-style structures

不仅……而且……

bù jǐn... ér qiě...

not only... but also...

一方面……另一方面……

yì fāng miàn... lìng yì fāng miàn...

on one hand... on the other hand...

合理使用

hé lǐ shǐ yòng

to use reasonably

保护隐私

bǎo hù yǐn sī

to protect privacy

互联网不仅方便，而且可以帮助学习。

The internet is not only convenient, but can also help studying.

我们应该合理使用手机，保护隐私。

We should use phones reasonably and protect privacy.

Final Check

Complete

我认为互联网很重要，因为它可以帮助学习。虽然手机很方便，但是会让人分心。我们应该合理使用手机，保护隐私。

I believe the internet is very important because it can help studying. Although phones are convenient, they can distract people. We should use phones reasonably and protect privacy.
