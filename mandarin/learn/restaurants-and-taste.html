Restaurants and Taste

Learn how to talk about restaurants, menus, taste and ordering food.

1. Places to eat

这儿

zhèr

here

餐厅

cāntīng

restaurant

小吃店

xiǎochīdiàn

snack shop

博客

bókè

blog

这儿有一个餐厅。

There is a restaurant here.

Quick Check

2. Describing taste

好吃

hǎochī

tasty, for food

好喝

hǎohē

tasty, for drinks

咸

xián

salty

甜

tián

sweet

美味可口

měiwèi kěkǒu

delicious

这个菜很好吃，但是有点儿咸。

This dish is tasty, but a bit salty.

果汁很好喝。

The fruit juice tastes good.

Check

3. Ordering food

菜单

càidān

menu

套餐

tàocān

set meal

盘

pán

plate

杯

bēi

cup / glass

甜点

tiándiǎn

dessert

请给我菜单。

Please give me the menu.

我要一杯茶和一盘点心。

I would like a cup of tea and a plate of snacks.

4. Dishes

叉烧包

chāshāobāo

barbecue pork bun

烧卖

shāomài

siu mai dumpling

虾

xiā

prawn / shrimp

包

bāo

bun / bag

汉堡包

hànbǎobāo

hamburger

爱吃

ài chī

love eating

我爱吃叉烧包和烧卖。

I love eating barbecue pork buns and siu mai.

汉堡包很好吃。

Hamburgers taste good.

Final Check

Complete

这儿有一个餐厅。我爱吃叉烧包和烧卖，因为很好吃。我要一杯茶和一盘点心。

There is a restaurant here. I love eating barbecue pork buns and siu mai because they taste good. I would like a cup of tea and a plate of snacks.
