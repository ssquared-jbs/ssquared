Skills and Reflection

Learn how to explain what you gained from work experience and how it helped your future.

1. Skills from work

技能

jì néng

skills

沟通能力

gōu tōng néng lì

communication skills

团队合作

tuán duì hé zuò

teamwork

责任感

zé rèn gǎn

sense of responsibility

实习提高了我的沟通能力。

The work experience improved my communication skills.

我学会了团队合作。

I learned teamwork.

Quick Check

2. Reflecting on experience

学到了

xué dào le

learned / gained

明白了

míng bai le

understood / realised

经验

jīng yàn

experience

对我有帮助

duì wǒ yǒu bāng zhù

helpful for me

我学到了很多工作经验。

I gained a lot of work experience.

这次实习对我有帮助。

This work experience was helpful for me.

Check

3. Future links

将来

jiāng lái

in the future

职业

zhí yè

career / profession

申请工作

shēn qǐng gōng zuò

to apply for a job

更有信心

gèng yǒu xìn xīn

more confident

这个经验让我更有信心。

This experience made me more confident.

将来申请工作的时候，这个经验很有用。

When applying for jobs in the future, this experience will be useful.

4. Strong exam-style structures

不仅……而且……

bù jǐn... ér qiě...

not only... but also...

虽然……但是……

suī rán... dàn shì...

although... but...

因为……所以……

yīn wèi... suǒ yǐ...

because... so...

最重要的是

zuì zhòng yào de shì

most importantly

这次实习不仅有意思，而且对我的将来有帮助。

This work experience was not only interesting, but also helpful for my future.

虽然工作很累，但是我学到了很多。

Although the work was tiring, I learned a lot.

Final Check

Complete

虽然工作很累，但是我学到了很多。这次实习不仅提高了我的沟通能力，而且让我更有信心。最重要的是，这个经验对我的将来有帮助。

Although the work was tiring, I learned a lot. This work experience not only improved my communication skills, but also made me more confident. Most importantly, this experience is helpful for my future.
