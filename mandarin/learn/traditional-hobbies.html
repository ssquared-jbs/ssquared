Traditional Hobbies and Sports

Learn how to talk about traditional activities, older people, learning hobbies and different sports.

1. Traditional activities

传统

chuántǒng

traditional

有的人

yǒu de rén

some people

老人

lǎorén

elderly person

有的人喜欢传统爱好。

Some people like traditional hobbies.

Quick check

2. Learning and fishing

学

xué

to learn / study

钓鱼

diàoyú

fishing

了

le

completed action marker

我学了钓鱼。

I learnt fishing.

Check

3. More sports

游泳

yóuyǒng

swimming

保龄球

bǎolíngqiú

bowling

滑雪

huáxuě

skiing

排

pái

row / line / arrange

我喜欢游泳，但是不喜欢滑雪。

I like swimming, but I do not like skiing.

老人有时候打保龄球。

Elderly people sometimes go bowling.

Final check

Complete

有的人喜欢传统爱好。老人有时候钓鱼。我喜欢游泳，也学了滑雪。

Some people like traditional hobbies. Elderly people sometimes fish. I like swimming and also learnt skiing.
