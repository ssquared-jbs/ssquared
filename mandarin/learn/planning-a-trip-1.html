Making Travel Plans

Learn how to talk about future travel plans, destinations and companions.

1. Future travel phrases

打算

dǎ suàn

to plan to

想

xiǎng

to want to

将来

jiāng lái

in the future

明年

míng nián

next year

明年我打算去中国。

Next year I plan to go to China.

将来我想去北京旅行。

In the future I want to travel to Beijing.

Quick Check

2. Talking about destinations

目的地

mù dì dì

destination

海边

hǎi biān

seaside

山区

shān qū

mountain area

国外

guó wài

abroad

我的目的地是上海。

My destination is Shanghai.

今年夏天我们想去海边。

This summer we want to go to the seaside.

Check

3. Travelling with people

跟

gēn

with

朋友

péng you

friend

家人

jiā rén

family

一个人

yí gè rén

alone

我打算跟家人一起旅行。

I plan to travel with my family.

我不想一个人去国外。

I do not want to go abroad alone.

4. Future intentions

希望

xī wàng

to hope

准备

zhǔn bèi

to prepare

学习

xué xí

to study

体验

tǐ yàn

to experience

我希望体验中国文化。

I hope to experience Chinese culture.

我准备学习很多新的东西。

I am preparing to learn many new things.

Final Check

Complete

明年我打算跟家人一起去中国旅行。我的目的地是北京。我希望体验中国文化，也想学习很多新的东西。

Next year I plan to travel to China with my family. My destination is Beijing. I hope to experience Chinese culture and also learn many new things.
