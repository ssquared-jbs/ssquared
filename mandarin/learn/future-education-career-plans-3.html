Career Ambitions

Learn how to talk about future jobs, ambitions and reasons for choosing a career.

1. Future jobs

医生

yī shēng

doctor

老师

lǎo shī

teacher

工程师

gōng chéng shī

engineer

律师

lǜ shī

lawyer

将来我想当医生。

In the future I want to be a doctor.

我的朋友想当工程师。

My friend wants to be an engineer.

Quick Check

2. Career words

职业

zhí yè

career / profession

工作

gōng zuò

job / work

工资

gōng zī

salary

机会

jī huì

opportunity

这个职业很有意思。

This career is very interesting.

我希望将来有一个好工作。

I hope to have a good job in the future.

Check

3. Reasons for career choices

帮助别人

bāng zhù bié rén

to help others

感兴趣

gǎn xìng qù

interested in

有挑战性

yǒu tiǎo zhàn xìng

challenging

有意义

yǒu yì yì

meaningful

我想当医生，因为我想帮助别人。

I want to be a doctor because I want to help others.

我对这个职业很感兴趣。

I am very interested in this career.

4. Ambitions

梦想

mèng xiǎng

dream / ambition

目标

mù biāo

goal

努力

nǔ lì

to work hard

实现

shí xiàn

to achieve / realise

我的梦想是当一名老师。

My dream is to become a teacher.

我会努力实现我的目标。

I will work hard to achieve my goal.

Final Check

Complete

将来我想当医生，因为我想帮助别人。我对这个职业很感兴趣。我的目标是努力学习，实现我的梦想。

In the future I want to be a doctor because I want to help others. I am very interested in this career. My goal is to study hard and achieve my dream.
