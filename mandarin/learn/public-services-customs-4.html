Complex Service Situations

Use reasons, polite phrases and contrast structures to explain public service problems clearly.

1. Polite requests

请问

qǐng wèn

excuse me / may I ask

可以帮我吗？

kě yǐ bāng wǒ ma?

Can you help me?

麻烦你了

má fan nǐ le

sorry to trouble you

谢谢你的帮助

xiè xie nǐ de bāng zhù

thank you for your help

请问，你可以帮我吗？

Excuse me, can you help me?

麻烦你了，谢谢你的帮助。

Sorry to trouble you, thank you for your help.

Quick Check

2. Giving reasons

因为

yīn wèi

because

所以

suǒ yǐ

so / therefore

需要

xū yào

to need

重要

zhòng yào

important

因为我的护照丢了，所以我需要帮助。

Because my passport is lost, I need help.

护照很重要，所以我很着急。

The passport is important, so I am very worried.

Check

3. Using contrast

虽然

suī rán

although

但是

dàn shì

but

可是

kě shì

however

最后

zuì hòu

finally

虽然我很着急，但是工作人员帮助了我。

Although I was worried, the staff helped me.

虽然问题很麻烦，可是最后解决了。

Although the problem was troublesome, it was finally solved.

4. Strong service sentences

不仅……而且……

bù jǐn... ér qiě...

not only... but also...

顺利

shùn lì

smoothly / successful

解决问题

jiě jué wèn tí

to solve a problem

让我放心

ràng wǒ fàng xīn

made me feel reassured

工作人员不仅友好，而且很快解决了问题。

The staff member was not only friendly, but also solved the problem quickly.

这让我放心了。

This made me feel reassured.

Final Check

Complete

因为我的护照丢了，所以我需要帮助。虽然我很着急，但是工作人员不仅友好，而且很快解决了问题。这让我放心了。

Because my passport was lost, I needed help. Although I was worried, the staff member was not only friendly, but also solved the problem quickly. This made me feel reassured.
