Seasons and Temperature

Learn how to compare weather, describe seasons and talk about extreme weather.

1. Comparing weather

……比……

bǐ

used for comparison

最

zuì

most

凉快

liáng kuai

cool

在A和B之间

zài A hé B zhī jiān

between A and B

上海比西安热。

Shanghai is hotter than Xi’an.

秋天最凉快。

Autumn is the coolest.

Quick Check

2. Rain and extreme weather

雨衣

yǔ yī

raincoat

零下

líng xià

below zero

毛毛雨

máo mao yǔ

drizzle

雷雨

léi yǔ

thunderstorm

今天有毛毛雨，所以我要穿雨衣。

There is drizzle today, so I need to wear a raincoat.

冬天气温零下五度。

In winter, the temperature is five degrees below zero.

Check

3. Seasons

季节

jì jié

season

春天

chūn tiān

spring

秋天

qiū tiān

autumn

冬天

dōng tiān

winter

夜里

yè lǐ

at night

我最喜欢春天，因为天气不冷也不热。

I like spring the most because the weather is neither cold nor hot.

4. Clothes for weather

强风

qiáng fēng

strong wind

毛衣

máo yī

jumper / sweater

夜里有强风，所以我要穿毛衣。

At night there is strong wind, so I need to wear a jumper.

Final Check

Complete

我最喜欢秋天，因为秋天最凉快。冬天气温有时候零下，所以我要穿毛衣。

I like autumn the most because autumn is the coolest. In winter, the temperature is sometimes below zero, so I need to wear a jumper.
