Past Travel Experiences

Learn how to talk about past trips using time phrases and 了.

1. Time phrases

去年

qù nián

last year

上个月

shàng gè yuè

last month

去年夏天

qù nián xià tiān

last summer

上个星期

shàng gè xīng qī

last week

去年我去了北京。

Last year I went to Beijing.

去年夏天我去了中国。

Last summer I went to China.

Quick Check

2. Using 了 for past actions

了

le

past marker

去了

qù le

went

参观了

cān guān le

visited

看了

kàn le

saw

我参观了很多景点。

I visited many attractions.

我看了长城。

I saw the Great Wall.

Check

3. Sequencing events

先

xiān

first

然后

rán hòu

then

最后

zuì hòu

finally

以后

yǐ hòu

after

我先去了北京，然后去了上海。

I first went to Beijing, then went to Shanghai.

参观以后，我们去吃饭。

After visiting, we went to eat.

4. Duration and frequency

两天

liǎng tiān

two days

一个星期

yí gè xīng qī

one week

每天

měi tiān

every day

一次

yí cì

once

我在中国待了一个星期。

I stayed in China for one week.

我每天都去参观景点。

I visited attractions every day.

Final Check

Complete

去年夏天我去了中国。我先去了北京，然后去了上海。我参观了很多景点，还吃了很多美食。我在那里待了一个星期。

Last summer I went to China. I first went to Beijing, then went to Shanghai. I visited many attractions and also ate lots of delicious food. I stayed there for one week.
