Describing Facilities

Learn how to describe what your school has and give basic opinions about facilities.

1. Saying what your school has

我的学校有……

wǒ de xué xiào yǒu...

My school has...

我的学校没有……

wǒ de xué xiào méi yǒu...

My school does not have...

设备

shè bèi

equipment

设施

shè shī

facilities

我的学校有很多现代化的设施。

My school has lots of modern facilities.

我的学校没有游泳池。

My school does not have a swimming pool.

Quick Check

2. Describing quality

很好

hěn hǎo

very good

现代化

xiàn dài huà

modern

方便

fāng biàn

convenient

不方便

bù fāng biàn

inconvenient

实验室的设备很好。

The laboratory equipment is very good.

学校的设施很现代化。

The school facilities are very modern.

Check

3. Describing size and appearance

大

dà

big

小

xiǎo

small

漂亮

piào liang

beautiful / nice-looking

干净

gān jìng

clean

我们的体育馆很大。

Our sports hall is very big.

食堂很干净，也很漂亮。

The canteen is clean and also nice-looking.

4. Adding simple reasons

因为

yīn wèi

because

所以

suǒ yǐ

so / therefore

我喜欢……

wǒ xǐ huān...

I like...

我不喜欢……

wǒ bù xǐ huān...

I do not like...

我喜欢图书馆，因为那里很安静。

I like the library because it is quiet there.

食堂很方便，所以我每天在那里吃午饭。

The canteen is convenient, so I eat lunch there every day.

Final Check

Complete

我的学校有很多现代化的设施。实验室的设备很好，体育馆很大。我喜欢图书馆，因为那里很安静。

My school has lots of modern facilities. The laboratory equipment is very good, and the sports hall is big. I like the library because it is quiet there.
