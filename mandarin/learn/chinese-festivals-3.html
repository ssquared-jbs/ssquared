Festival Food and Activities

Learn how to describe festival meals, gifts, performances and celebrations.

1. Festival food

饺子

jiǎo zi

dumplings

月饼

yuè bǐng

mooncake

粽子

zòng zi

rice dumpling

年夜饭

nián yè fàn

New Year’s Eve dinner

春节的时候，我们吃饺子。

During Spring Festival, we eat dumplings.

中秋节的时候，人们吃月饼。

During Mid-Autumn Festival, people eat mooncakes.

2. Gifts and wishes

礼物

lǐ wù

gift

红包

hóng bāo

red envelope

祝福

zhù fú

blessing / good wishes

新年快乐

xīn nián kuài lè

Happy New Year

孩子们会收到红包。

Children receive red envelopes.

我们会说“新年快乐”。

We say “Happy New Year”.

Check

3. Festival activities

看表演

kàn biǎo yǎn

to watch performances

唱歌

chàng gē

to sing

跳舞

tiào wǔ

to dance

拍照

pāi zhào

to take photos

我们喜欢看表演和拍照。

We like watching performances and taking photos.

节日的时候，大家唱歌跳舞。

During festivals, everyone sings and dances.

4. Describing the experience

好吃

hǎo chī

delicious

热闹

rè nao

lively

有趣

yǒu qù

interesting / fun

难忘

nán wàng

unforgettable

年夜饭很好吃。

The New Year’s Eve dinner is delicious.

这个节日很热闹，也很难忘。

This festival is lively and unforgettable.

Final Check

Complete

春节的时候，我们吃饺子和年夜饭。孩子们会收到红包。我们也会看表演、唱歌和拍照。我觉得春节很热闹，也很难忘。

During Spring Festival, we eat dumplings and New Year’s Eve dinner. Children receive red envelopes. We also watch performances, sing and take photos. I think Spring Festival is lively and unforgettable.
