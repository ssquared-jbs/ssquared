Interview Questions

Learn how to ask celebrities questions about their lives, hobbies and success.

1. Basic interview questions

采访

cǎi fǎng

to interview

问题

wèn tí

question

回答

huí dá

to answer

请问

qǐng wèn

may I ask...

我想采访这位明星。

I want to interview this celebrity.

请问，你为什么想当歌手？

May I ask, why did you want to become a singer?

Quick Check

2. Asking about hobbies and interests

爱好

ài hào

hobby

空闲时间

kòng xián shí jiān

free time

喜欢做

xǐ huān zuò

like doing

平常

píng cháng

usually / normally

你空闲时间喜欢做什么？

What do you like doing in your free time?

你的爱好是什么？

What are your hobbies?

Check

3. Asking about success

成功

chéng gōng

success / successful

努力

nǔ lì

to work hard

困难

kùn nan

difficulty

梦想

mèng xiǎng

dream

你觉得成功最重要的是什么？

What do you think is most important for success?

你小时候的梦想是什么？

What was your dream when you were young?

4. Giving interview reactions

有意思

yǒu yì si

interesting

学到

xué dào

to learn from

佩服

pèi fu

to admire

经验

jīng yàn

experience

这次采访很有意思。

This interview was very interesting.

我从他的经验中学到了很多。

I learnt a lot from his experience.

Final Check

Complete

我采访了一位中国歌手。我问了很多问题，比如他的爱好和梦想。我觉得这次采访很有意思，我也学到了很多。

I interviewed a Chinese singer. I asked many questions, such as his hobbies and dreams. I think this interview was very interesting and I also learnt a lot.
