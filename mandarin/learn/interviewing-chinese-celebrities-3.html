Achievements and Fame

Learn how to describe success, awards, popularity and influence.

1. Achievements

成就

chéng jiù

achievement

成功

chéng gōng

success / successful

奖

jiǎng

award / prize

得奖

dé jiǎng

to win an award

这位演员有很多成就。

This actor has many achievements.

她去年得了一个奖。

She won an award last year.

Quick Check

2. Popularity

受欢迎

shòu huān yíng

popular

粉丝

fěn sī

fans

有名

yǒu míng

famous

出名

chū míng

to become famous

这个歌手很受欢迎。

This singer is very popular.

他有很多年轻粉丝。

He has many young fans.

Check

3. Influence

影响

yǐng xiǎng

influence / impact

榜样

bǎng yàng

role model

鼓励

gǔ lì

to encourage

年轻人

nián qīng rén

young people

她是很多年轻人的榜样。

She is a role model for many young people.

他的故事鼓励了很多人。

His story encouraged many people.

4. Giving opinions on fame

压力

yā lì

pressure / stress

不容易

bù róng yì

not easy

值得佩服

zhí de pèi fu

worth admiring

努力工作

nǔ lì gōng zuò

to work hard

我觉得当明星不容易。

I think being a celebrity is not easy.

她努力工作，值得佩服。

She works hard and is worth admiring.

Final Check

Complete

这位歌手很成功，也很受欢迎。她有很多粉丝，而且得过很多奖。我觉得她是年轻人的榜样，值得佩服。

This singer is very successful and popular. She has many fans and has won many awards. I think she is a role model for young people and is worth admiring.
