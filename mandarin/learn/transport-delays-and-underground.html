Delays and Underground

Learn how to talk about delays, underground travel, crowds and famous places.

1. Delays and arrival

到达

dào dá

to arrive

误点

wù diǎn

delay

出现

chū xiàn

to appear

凌晨

líng chén

before dawn

火车误点了，所以凌晨才到达。

The train was delayed, so it only arrived before dawn.

Quick Check

2. Crowds and traffic

人山人海

rén shān rén hǎi

sea of people

车水马龙

chē shuǐ mǎ lóng

heavy traffic

挤

jǐ

crowded

慢

màn

slow

角落

jiǎo luò

corner

周末地铁站人山人海，交通车水马龙。

At the weekend the underground station is packed, and the traffic is very heavy.

地铁很挤，而且很慢。

The underground is crowded and also slow.

Check

3. Underground and stations

地下

dì xià

underground

铁路

tiě lù

railway

站

zhàn

station / stop

出口

chū kǒu

exit

周末

zhōu mò

weekend

地铁站的出口很难找。

The exit of the underground station is hard to find.

4. Direct routes and places

直接

zhí jiē

directly

海德公园

Hǎi dé gōng yuán

Hyde Park

有名

yǒu míng

famous

你可以坐地铁直接去海德公园。

You can take the underground directly to Hyde Park.

海德公园很有名。

Hyde Park is very famous.

Final Check

Complete

周末地铁站人山人海，地铁又挤又慢。火车误点了，所以我凌晨才到达。

At the weekend the underground station was packed, and the underground was crowded and slow. The train was delayed, so I only arrived before dawn.
