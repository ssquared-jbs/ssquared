Meals and Restaurants

Learn how to talk about meals, restaurants, cooking, taste and eating with others.

1. Eating tools

用

yòng

to use

刀

dāo

knife

叉

chā

fork

木头

mùtou

wood

竹子

zhúzi

bamboo

我用刀和叉吃西餐。

I use a knife and fork to eat Western food.

Quick Check

2. Taste words

酸

suān

sour

苦

kǔ

bitter

饱

bǎo

full

酸辣汤

suānlà tāng

hot and sour soup

酸辣汤很好喝，但是有点儿酸。

Hot and sour soup tastes good, but it is a bit sour.

Check

3. Meals and cooking

餐

cān

meal

一日三餐

yí rì sān cān

three meals a day

做饭

zuò fàn

to cook

鱼

yú

fish

薯条

shǔtiáo

chips / fries

我爸爸喜欢做饭，也喜欢做鱼。

My dad likes cooking and also likes cooking fish.

我一日三餐都吃米饭。

I eat rice for all three meals a day.

4. Restaurants and food types

想

xiǎng

want / would like

一块儿

yíkuàir

together

饭馆

fànguǎn

restaurant

中餐

zhōngcān

Chinese food

西餐

xīcān

Western food

我想和朋友一块儿去饭馆吃中餐。

I would like to go to a restaurant with my friend to eat Chinese food.

Final Check

Complete

我想和朋友一块儿去饭馆吃中餐。我喜欢酸辣汤，也喜欢吃鱼和薯条。

I would like to go to a restaurant with my friend to eat Chinese food. I like hot and sour soup, and I also like eating fish and chips.
