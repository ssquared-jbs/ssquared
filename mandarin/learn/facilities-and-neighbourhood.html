Facilities and Neighbourhood

Learn how to describe local facilities and places near your home.

1. Public Facilities

设施

shè shī

facilities

电影院

cinema

体育场

tǐ yù chǎng

sports stadium

图书馆

tú shū guǎn

library

我家附近有图书馆和电影院。

There is a library and a cinema near my home.
