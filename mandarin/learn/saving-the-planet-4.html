Complex Environmental Opinions

Use reasons, contrasts and stronger structures to explain how we can protect the planet.

1. Giving opinions

我认为……

wǒ rèn wéi...

I believe...

我觉得……

wǒ jué de...

I think...

对我来说

duì wǒ lái shuō

for me

保护地球

bǎo hù dì qiú

to protect the planet

我认为保护地球很重要。

I believe protecting the planet is very important.

对我来说，环保是每个人的责任。

For me, environmental protection is everyone’s responsibility.

Quick Check

2. Giving reasons

因为

yīn wèi

because

所以

suǒ yǐ

so / therefore

影响未来

yǐng xiǎng wèi lái

to affect the future

减少污染

jiǎn shǎo wū rǎn

to reduce pollution

因为污染会影响未来，所以我们必须改变。

Because pollution will affect the future, we must change.

少开汽车可以减少污染。

Using cars less can reduce pollution.

Check

3. Using contrast

虽然

suī rán

although

但是

dàn shì

but

不方便

bù fāng biàn

inconvenient

有帮助

yǒu bāng zhù

helpful

虽然骑自行车有时候不方便，但是对环境有帮助。

Although riding a bike is sometimes inconvenient, it is helpful for the environment.

虽然环保不容易，但是非常重要。

Although protecting the environment is not easy, it is very important.

4. Strong environmental structures

不仅……而且……

bù jǐn... ér qiě...

not only... but also...

最重要的是

zuì zhòng yào de shì

most importantly

采取行动

cǎi qǔ xíng dòng

to take action

改变生活方式

gǎi biàn shēng huó fāng shì

to change lifestyle

环保不仅对地球有帮助，而且对未来很重要。

Environmental protection is not only helpful for the planet, but also important for the future.

最重要的是，我们应该采取行动。

Most importantly, we should take action.

Final Check

Complete

我认为保护地球很重要，因为污染会影响未来。虽然环保有时候不方便，但是对环境很有帮助。环保不仅对地球有帮助，而且对未来很重要。最重要的是，我们应该采取行动。

I believe protecting the planet is very important because pollution will affect the future. Although protecting the environment is sometimes inconvenient, it is very helpful for the environment. Environmental protection is not only helpful for the planet, but also important for the future. Most importantly, we should take action.
