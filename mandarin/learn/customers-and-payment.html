Customers and Payment

Learn shopping words for customers, wallets, mobile phones, bags, discounts and payment.

1. Customers and belongings

顾客

gù kè

customer

钱包

qián bāo

wallet

手机

shǒu jī

mobile phone

书包

shū bāo

school bag

顾客有钱包、手机和书包。

The customer has a wallet, mobile phone and school bag.

Quick Check

2. Payment and discounts

减价

jiǎn jià

price reduction / discount

付钱

fù qián

to pay

这个手机减价了。

This mobile phone has been reduced in price.

我用钱包里的钱付钱。

I use the money in my wallet to pay.

Final Check

Complete

顾客想买手机。这个手机减价了，所以他用钱包里的钱付钱。

The customer wants to buy a mobile phone. This mobile phone has a discount, so he uses the money in his wallet to pay.
