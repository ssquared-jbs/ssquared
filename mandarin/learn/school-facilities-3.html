Open Days and Visits

Learn how to describe school open days, visits, routes and guided explanations.

1. Open day vocabulary

开放日

kāi fàng rì

open day

参观

cān guān

to visit / tour

家长

jiā zhǎng

parents / guardians

学生

xué sheng

student

上个星期，我们学校有开放日。

Last week, our school had an open day.

很多家长来参观学校。

Many parents came to visit the school.

Quick Check

2. Routes around school

路线

lù xiàn

route

带

dài

to lead / take someone

先

xiān

first

然后

rán hòu

then

老师带家长参观教学楼。

The teacher led the parents around the teaching building.

我们先参观实验室，然后去体育馆。

We first visited the laboratory, then went to the sports hall.

Check

3. Explaining school facilities

讲解

jiǎng jiě

to explain

介绍

jiè shào

to introduce

校长

xiào zhǎng

head teacher / principal

特色

tè sè

special feature

校长介绍了学校的设施。

The head teacher introduced the school facilities.

学生讲解了学校的特色。

The students explained the school’s special features.

4. Giving opinions about visits

有意思

yǒu yì si

interesting

很有用

hěn yǒu yòng

very useful

了解

liǎo jiě

to understand / learn about

印象

yìn xiàng

impression

我觉得开放日很有用。

I think the open day was very useful.

实验室给我留下了深刻的印象。

The laboratory left a deep impression on me.

Final Check

Complete

上个星期，我们学校有开放日。老师带家长参观教学楼、实验室和体育馆。校长介绍了学校的设施。我觉得开放日很有用。

Last week, our school had an open day. The teacher led parents around the teaching building, laboratory and sports hall. The head teacher introduced the school facilities. I think the open day was very useful.
