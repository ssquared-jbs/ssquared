Medicine and Recovery

Learn how to talk about symptoms, medicine, sick notes, health and recovery.

1. Symptoms and illness

咳嗽

ké sou

to cough

多

duō

many / more

少

shǎo

few / less

怎么了

zěn me le

what happened?

你怎么了？我咳嗽了。

What happened? I have a cough.

Quick Check

2. Medicine and treatment

打针

dǎ zhēn

injection

吃药

chī yào

take medicine

药

yào

medicine

次

cì

time / occasion

西药

xī yào

Western medicine

医生说我要吃药，一天三次。

The doctor says I need to take medicine three times a day.

我不喜欢打针。

I do not like injections.

Check

3. Help and recovery

帮助

bāng zhù

to help

大夫

dài fu

doctor

原谅

yuán liàng

to forgive

早日康复

zǎo rì kāng fù

get better soon

大夫帮助我看病。

The doctor helps me with my illness.

祝你早日康复。

I wish you a speedy recovery.

4. Sick notes and messages

收件人

shōu jiàn rén

recipient

发件人

fā jiàn rén

sender

主题

zhǔ tí

subject / topic

病假条

bìng jià tiáo

sick note

请假

qǐng jià

ask for leave

我生病了，所以要请假。

I am ill, so I need to ask for leave.

这个邮件的主题是病假条。

The subject of this email is sick note.

5. Appearance and health words

美

měi

beautiful / pretty

难看

nán kàn

not good-looking

胃

wèi

stomach

健康

jiàn kāng

health / healthy

零食

líng shí

snack

丑

chǒu

ugly

少吃零食对健康很好。

Eating fewer snacks is good for health.

我的胃不舒服。

My stomach does not feel well.

6. Hope and frequency

希望

xī wàng

to hope

经常

jīng cháng

often

长

zhǎng

to grow

上个星期

shàng ge xīng qī

last week

我希望你早日康复。

I hope you get better soon.

我经常锻炼身体。

I often exercise.

Final Check

Complete

我上个星期生病了，咳嗽得很厉害。大夫说我要吃药，一天三次。我希望早日康复。

Last week I was ill and had a bad cough. The doctor said I needed to take medicine three times a day. I hope to get better soon.
