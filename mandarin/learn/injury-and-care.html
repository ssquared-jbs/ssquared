Injury and Care

Learn how to talk about injuries, emergency care, appearance and kindness.

1. Injury words

手指

shǒu zhǐ

finger

受伤

shòu shāng

to get injured

流血

liú xuè

to bleed

严重

yán zhòng

serious / severe

我的手指受伤了，还流血了。

My finger was injured and bled.

Quick Check

2. Hospital and emergency care

救护车

jiù hù chē

ambulance

急救中心

jí jiù zhōng xīn

emergency centre

出院

chū yuàn

to leave hospital

病历卡

bìng lì kǎ

hospital report

症状

zhèng zhuàng

symptom

情况很严重，所以叫了救护车。

The situation was serious, so an ambulance was called.

医生看了我的病历卡和症状。

The doctor looked at my hospital report and symptoms.

Check

3. Appearance and care

年轻

nián qīng

young

皮肤

pí fū

skin

漂亮

piào liang

beautiful

心

xīn

heart

爱心

ài xīn

love / kindness

护士很有爱心，也很漂亮。

The nurse is very kind and also beautiful.

年轻人的皮肤很好。

Young people’s skin is very good.

Final Check

Complete

我的手指受伤了，还流血了。情况很严重，所以去了急救中心。护士很有爱心。

My finger was injured and bled. The situation was serious, so I went to the emergency centre. The nurse was very kind.
