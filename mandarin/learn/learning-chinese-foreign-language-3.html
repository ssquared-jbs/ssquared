Challenges and Progress

Learn how to describe difficulties, improvement and study progress in Mandarin.

1. Difficulties

困难

kùn nán

difficulty / difficult

复杂

fù zá

complicated

记不住

jì bu zhù

cannot remember

错误

cuò wù

mistake

我觉得汉字有一点复杂。

I think Chinese characters are a little complicated.

有时候我记不住新单词。

Sometimes I cannot remember new vocabulary.

Quick Check

2. Improving skills

提高

tí gāo

to improve

练习口语

liàn xí kǒu yǔ

to practise speaking

复习

fù xí

to revise

继续

jì xù

to continue

我每天练习口语来提高中文。

I practise speaking every day to improve my Chinese.

考试以前，我会复习。

Before exams, I revise.

Check

3. Future progress

希望

xī wàng

to hope

将来

jiāng lái

future

更好

gèng hǎo

better

目标

mù biāo

goal

我希望将来中文会更好。

I hope my Chinese will be better in the future.

我的目标是说流利的中文。

My goal is to speak fluent Chinese.

4. Positive study attitudes

坚持

jiān chí

to persist

成功

chéng gōng

success

耐心

nài xīn

patience

努力学习

nǔ lì xué xí

to study hard

学中文需要耐心。

Learning Chinese requires patience.

如果我坚持，我会成功。

If I persist, I will succeed.

Final Check

Complete

我觉得学中文有时候很困难，因为汉字有一点复杂。但是我每天练习口语，也会复习。我希望将来中文会更好。虽然学习中文需要耐心，但是如果我坚持，我会成功。

I think learning Chinese is sometimes difficult because Chinese characters are a little complicated. However, I practise speaking every day and also revise. I hope my Chinese will be better in the future. Although learning Chinese requires patience, if I persist, I will succeed.
