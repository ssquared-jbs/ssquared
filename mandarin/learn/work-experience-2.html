Tasks and Duties

Learn how to describe what you did during work experience and how you helped.

1. Daily work tasks

工作

gōng zuò

to work / work

帮忙

bāng máng

to help

整理

zhěng lǐ

to organise

打电话

dǎ diàn huà

to make phone calls

我帮忙整理文件。

I helped organise documents.

我每天都要打电话。

I had to make phone calls every day.

Quick Check

2. Helping customers and staff

帮助顾客

bāng zhù gù kè

to help customers

回答问题

huí dá wèn tí

to answer questions

服务

fú wù

service / to serve

介绍

jiè shào

to introduce / explain

我帮助顾客回答问题。

I helped customers answer questions.

经理介绍了公司的工作。

The manager explained the company’s work.

Check

3. Learning new skills

学习

xué xí

to learn

技能

jì néng

skill

经验

jīng yàn

experience

提高

tí gāo

to improve

我学习了很多新技能。

I learned many new skills.

这个经验提高了我的中文。

This experience improved my Chinese.

4. Talking about responsibility

负责

fù zé

to be responsible for

完成

wán chéng

to complete

准时

zhǔn shí

on time

认真

rèn zhēn

serious / hardworking

我负责整理办公室。

I was responsible for organising the office.

我认真完成了工作。

I completed the work seriously and carefully.

Final Check

Complete

在实习的时候，我帮助顾客回答问题，也负责整理办公室。我学习了很多新技能，这个经验提高了我的中文。

During work experience, I helped customers answer questions and was also responsible for organising the office. I learned many new skills and this experience improved my Chinese.
