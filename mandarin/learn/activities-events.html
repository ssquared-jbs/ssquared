Activities and Events

Learn how to talk about going places, birthdays, school events and playing badminton.

1. Going to events

去

qù

to go

生日会

shēngrì huì

birthday party

一起

yìqǐ

together

我们一起去生日会。

We go to the birthday party together.

Quick check

2. Sport and school

打

dǎ

to play / hit

羽毛球

yǔmáoqiú

badminton

学校

xuéxiào

school

我在学校打羽毛球。

I play badminton at school.

Check

3. Performances and answers

演出

yǎnchū

performance

对

duì

correct / right

错

cuò

wrong

这个回答是对的。

This answer is correct.

学校有一个演出。

The school has a performance.

Final check

Complete

我们一起去学校。下午我打羽毛球，晚上有一个演出。

We go to school together. In the afternoon I play badminton, and in the evening there is a performance.
