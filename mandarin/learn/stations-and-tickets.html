Stations and Timetables

Learn how to talk about airports, timetables, journeys, delays and traffic jams.

1. Airport and schedules

飞机场

fēi jī chǎng

airport

准时

zhǔn shí

on time

起飞

qǐ fēi

to take off

时刻表

shí kè biǎo

schedule / timetable

飞机准时起飞。

The plane takes off on time.

我看了飞机场的时刻表。

I looked at the airport timetable.

Quick Check

2. Journey details

路程

lù chéng

journey

路

lù

route number

大概

dà gài

probably / roughly

更

gèng

even more

很少

hěn shǎo

seldom / rarely

这个路程大概一个小时。

This journey is roughly one hour.

我很少坐这路车。

I rarely take this bus route.

Check

3. Work and daily travel

上班

shàng bān

to go to work

下班

xià bān

to get off work

回家

huí jiā

to go home

晚

wǎn

late

末班车

mò bān chē

last bus / train

我下班以后坐末班车回家。

After getting off work, I take the last bus home.

4. Busy transport and delays

热闹

rè nào

busy and lively

堵车

dǔ chē

traffic jam

迟到

chí dào

to be late

因为堵车，所以我迟到了。

Because of traffic, I was late.

火车站很热闹。

The train station is very busy and lively.

Final Check

Complete

我看了时刻表。飞机准时起飞，可是因为堵车，我到飞机场迟到了。

I checked the timetable. The plane took off on time, but because of traffic, I arrived late at the airport.
