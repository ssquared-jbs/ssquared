Transport Opinions

Learn how to describe transport methods and give opinions about travel.

1. Extra transport methods

高铁

gāo tiě

high-speed rail

骑自行车

qí zì xíng chē

to ride a bike

我喜欢坐高铁，因为很快。

I like taking high-speed rail because it is fast.

我每天骑自行车去学校。

I ride a bike to school every day.

Quick Check

2. Describing transport

形容

xíng róng

to describe

请你形容一下你最喜欢的交通工具。

Please describe your favourite means of transport.

我觉得高铁又快又方便。

I think high-speed rail is both fast and convenient.

Final Check

Complete

我最喜欢坐高铁，因为高铁很快，也很方便。有时候我骑自行车去学校。

I like taking high-speed rail the most because it is fast and convenient. Sometimes I ride a bike to school.
