Environmental Problems

Learn vocabulary for pollution, rubbish, climate problems and environmental damage.

1. Pollution

污染

wū rǎn

pollution

空气污染

kōng qì wū rǎn

air pollution

垃圾

lā jī

rubbish / litter

塑料

sù liào

plastic

空气污染越来越严重。

Air pollution is becoming more and more serious.

很多塑料垃圾进入海洋。

A lot of plastic rubbish enters the ocean.

Quick Check

2. Climate and nature

气候变化

qì hòu biàn huà

climate change

地球

dì qiú

Earth / planet

森林

sēn lín

forest

动物

dòng wù

animal

气候变化影响很多动物。

Climate change affects many animals.

保护森林很重要。

Protecting forests is very important.

Check

3. Environmental damage

浪费

làng fèi

to waste

能源

néng yuán

energy

水

shuǐ

water

危险

wēi xiǎn

dangerous

浪费能源对地球不好。

Wasting energy is bad for the planet.

污染对动物很危险。

Pollution is dangerous for animals.

4. Opinions about the environment

重要

zhòng yào

important

严重

yán zhòng

serious

保护环境

bǎo hù huán jìng

to protect the environment

未来

wèi lái

future

我觉得保护环境很重要。

I think protecting the environment is very important.

如果我们不改变，未来会很危险。

If we do not change, the future will be very dangerous.

Final Check

Complete

空气污染越来越严重。很多塑料垃圾进入海洋，也影响动物。我觉得保护环境很重要，因为浪费能源对地球不好。如果我们不改变，未来会很危险。

Air pollution is becoming more and more serious. A lot of plastic rubbish enters the ocean and also affects animals. I think protecting the environment is very important because wasting energy is bad for the planet. If we do not change, the future will be very dangerous.
