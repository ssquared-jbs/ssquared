Asking for Directions

Learn how to ask where places are, how far they are, and how to get there.

1. Asking where and how far

……离……有多远？

... lí ... yǒu duō yuǎn?

How far is ... from ...?

……在哪儿？

... zài nǎr?

Where is ...?

目的地

mù dì dì

destination

公用电话

gōng yòng diàn huà

public phone

图书馆离我家有多远？

How far is the library from my home?

电影院在哪儿？

Where is the cinema?

Quick Check

2. Getting somewhere

到……怎么走？

dào ... zěn me zǒu?

How do you get to ...?

出去

chū qù

to go out

往……走

wǎng ... zǒu

go towards / in the direction of

路过

lù guò

to pass through / pass by

到体育场怎么走？

How do you get to the sports stadium?

你出去以后，往南走。

After you go out, walk south.

Check

3. Giving directions

过马路

guò mǎ lù

to cross the road

一直

yì zhí

straight / all the way

顺着

shùn zhe

along

在……边

zài ... biān

on the side

过马路以后，一直走。

After crossing the road, go straight ahead.

顺着这条路走，邮局在右边。

Go along this road. The post office is on the right side.

4. Problems with directions

迷路

mí lù

to get lost

麻烦你了

má fan nǐ le

sorry to bother you

我迷路了，麻烦你了。

I am lost, sorry to bother you.

Final Check

Complete

到图书馆怎么走？出去以后，过马路，然后一直走。图书馆在右边。

How do you get to the library? After going out, cross the road, then go straight. The library is on the right.
