Travel and Journeys

Learn how to talk about journeys, tickets, taxis and travel plans.

1. Taxi and travel

出租车

chū zū chē

taxi

打车

dǎ chē

to take a taxi

开车

kāi chē

to drive

我今天打车上班。

Today I take a taxi to work.

他开车去学校。

He drives to school.

Quick Check

2. Sequence (first… then…)

先……然后……

xiān...rán hòu...

first... then...

我先坐车，然后走路。

I first take transport, then walk.

Check

3. Train travel

火车站

huǒ chē zhàn

train station

售票员

shòu piào yuán

ticket agent

出发

chū fā

to set out

一张票

yì zhāng piào

a ticket

单程票

dān chéng piào

single ticket

来回票

lái huí piào

return ticket

我要买一张来回票。

I want to buy a return ticket.

4. Travel items

行李

xíng li

luggage

软卧

ruǎn wò

soft sleeper

硬卧

yìng wò

hard sleeper

小岛

xiǎo dǎo

island

我带很多行李去小岛。

I bring a lot of luggage to the island.

Final Check

Complete

我先打车去火车站，然后买一张来回票。我带行李去一个小岛旅行。

I first take a taxi to the train station, then buy a return ticket. I take luggage to travel to an island.
