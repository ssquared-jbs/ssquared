Symptoms and Descriptions

Learn how to describe people, illness, phone calls and appointments.

1. Describing appearance

矮

ǎi

short, height

胖

pàng

fat

瘦

shòu

slim

长

cháng

long

短

duǎn

short, length

我的哥哥很高，我的弟弟很矮。

My older brother is tall, and my younger brother is short.

Quick Check

2. Feeling ill

生病

shēng bìng

to get sick

舒服

shū fu

comfortable

不舒服

bù shū fu

not well

嗓子

sǎng zi

throat

可以

kě yǐ

can / may

我生病了，嗓子不舒服。

I am ill, and my throat does not feel well.

Check

3. Phone calls and appointments

打电话

dǎ diàn huà

make a phone call

喂

wéi

hello, on the phone

给

gěi

to give

护士

hù shi

nurse

预约

yù yuē

make an appointment / book

我给医院打电话，想预约看病。

I phone the hospital and want to book an appointment to see a doctor.

喂，请问护士在吗？

Hello, is the nurse there?

Final Check

Complete

我生病了，嗓子不舒服。我给医院打电话，想预约看病。

I am ill and my throat does not feel well. I phone the hospital and want to book an appointment.
