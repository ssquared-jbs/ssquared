Learning Chinese

Learn vocabulary for studying Chinese, lessons and language practice.

1. Studying Chinese

学中文

xué zhōng wén

to learn Chinese

汉语

hàn yǔ

Chinese language / Mandarin

老师

lǎo shī

teacher

学生

xué sheng

student

我在学校学中文。

I learn Chinese at school.

我的中文老师很好。

My Chinese teacher is very good.

Quick Check

2. Lessons and homework

上课

shàng kè

to attend lessons

作业

zuò yè

homework

考试

kǎo shì

exam

练习

liàn xí

to practise

我每天做中文作业。

I do Chinese homework every day.

我常常练习汉语。

I often practise Mandarin.

Check

3. Learning methods

听音乐

tīng yīn yuè

to listen to music

看视频

kàn shì pín

to watch videos

读书

dú shū

to read books

记单词

jì dān cí

to memorise vocabulary

我喜欢看中文视频。

I like watching Chinese videos.

我每天记中文单词。

I memorise Chinese vocabulary every day.

4. Basic opinions

有意思

yǒu yì si

interesting

重要

zhòng yào

important

难

nán

difficult

容易

róng yì

easy

我觉得学中文很有意思。

I think learning Chinese is very interesting.

汉语不容易，但是很重要。

Mandarin is not easy, but it is important.

Final Check

Complete

我在学校学中文。我每天做作业，也常常练习汉语。我喜欢看中文视频和记单词。我觉得学中文很有意思，但是不容易。

I learn Chinese at school. I do homework every day and often practise Mandarin. I like watching Chinese videos and memorising vocabulary. I think learning Chinese is very interesting, but not easy.
