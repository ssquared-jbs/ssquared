Learn how to talk about music, hobbies, opinions and events.

1. Music hobbies

guitar

钢琴

to play, string/key instrument

2. Opinions

to think / feel

有意思

interesting

没有意思

好玩儿

我觉得弹吉他很有意思。

I think playing guitar is very interesting.

我觉得小说没有意思。

I think novels are boring.

Final check

我会弹钢琴，也喜欢弹吉他。我觉得音乐很有意思，运动会也很好玩儿。

I can play piano and also like playing guitar. I think music is interesting, and sports day is also fun.
