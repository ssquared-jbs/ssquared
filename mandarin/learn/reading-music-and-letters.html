Reading, Music and Letters

Learn how to talk about reading, music, writing letters and outdoor hobbies.

1. Reading materials

杂志

zázhì

magazine

小人书

xiǎorénshū

comic book

连环画

liánhuánhuà

comic strip / picture story

我喜欢看杂志和小人书。

I like reading magazines and comic books.

Quick check

2. Letters and pen friends

写信

xiě xìn

write a letter

笔友

bǐyǒu

pen friend

亲爱的

qīn'ài de

dear

回信

huí xìn

reply to a letter

亲爱的笔友，我喜欢写信。

Dear pen friend, I like writing letters.

Check

3. Music and outdoor hobbies

古典音乐

gǔdiǎn yīnyuè

classical music

流行歌曲

liúxíng gēqǔ

pop song

爬山

pá shān

climb mountains / hiking

踢足球

tī zúqiú

play football

我喜欢听流行歌曲。

I like listening to pop songs.

周末我常常爬山，也踢足球。

At weekends I often go hiking and also play football.

4. Polite phrases

不客气

bú kèqi

you are welcome

祝

zhù

wish / wish you

不客气！祝你快乐。

You are welcome! I wish you happiness.

Final check

Complete

亲爱的笔友，我喜欢看杂志，也喜欢听流行歌曲。周末我常常爬山和踢足球。

Dear pen friend, I like reading magazines and also like listening to pop songs. At weekends I often go hiking and play football.
