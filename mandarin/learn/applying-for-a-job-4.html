Complex Job Answers

Use reasons, contrast phrases and stronger structures to improve job application answers.

1. Explaining suitability

适合

shì hé

suitable / to suit

能力

néng lì

ability

经验

jīng yàn

experience

责任感

zé rèn gǎn

sense of responsibility

我觉得这个工作很适合我。

I think this job suits me well.

我有经验，也有责任感。

I have experience and also a sense of responsibility.

Quick Check

2. Giving reasons

因为

yīn wèi

because

所以

suǒ yǐ

so / therefore

想提高

xiǎng tí gāo

want to improve

将来

jiāng lái

future

因为我想提高我的沟通能力，所以我想申请这个工作。

Because I want to improve my communication skills, I want to apply for this job.

这个机会对我的将来很有帮助。

This opportunity is very helpful for my future.

Check

3. Using contrast

虽然

suī rán

although

但是

dàn shì

but

紧张

jǐn zhāng

nervous

准备好了

zhǔn bèi hǎo le

prepared / ready

虽然我有点儿紧张，但是我准备好了。

Although I was a little nervous, I was prepared.

虽然我没有很多经验，但是我很努力。

Although I do not have lots of experience, I am hardworking.

4. Strong application structures

不仅……而且……

bù jǐn... ér qiě...

not only... but also...

如果有机会

rú guǒ yǒu jī huì

if I have the chance

更有信心

gèng yǒu xìn xīn

more confident

成功

chéng gōng

success / successful

我不仅认真，而且很可靠。

I am not only hardworking, but also reliable.

如果有机会，我会努力工作。

If I have the chance, I will work hard.

Final Check

Complete

我觉得这个工作很适合我。因为我想提高我的沟通能力，所以我想申请这个工作。虽然我没有很多经验，但是我很努力。我不仅认真，而且很可靠。

I think this job suits me well. Because I want to improve my communication skills, I want to apply for this job. Although I do not have lots of experience, I am hardworking. I am not only serious, but also reliable.
