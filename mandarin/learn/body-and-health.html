3. Health basics

身体

shēn tǐ

body

吸烟

xī yān

smoking

对…好

duì...hǎo

good for

对…不好

duì...bùhǎo

not good for

运动对身体很好。

Exercise is good for the body.

吸烟对身体不好。

Smoking is bad for the body.

Final Check

Complete

我有头、手和脚。运动对身体很好，但是吸烟对身体不好。

I have a head, hands and feet. Exercise is good for the body, but smoking is bad for the body.
