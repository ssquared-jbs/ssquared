Important Festivals

Learn vocabulary for major Chinese festivals and celebrations.

1. Major Chinese festivals

春节

chūn jié

Spring Festival / Chinese New Year

中秋节

zhōng qiū jié

Mid-Autumn Festival

端午节

duān wǔ jié

Dragon Boat Festival

节日

jié rì

festival

春节是中国最重要的节日。

Spring Festival is the most important festival in China.

我很喜欢中秋节。

I really like the Mid-Autumn Festival.

Quick Check

2. Celebrations

庆祝

qìng zhù

to celebrate

放烟花

fàng yān huā

to set off fireworks

看舞龙

kàn wǔ lóng

to watch dragon dances

热闹

rè nao

lively

很多人春节的时候放烟花。

Many people set off fireworks during Spring Festival.

春节非常热闹。

Spring Festival is very lively.

Check

3. Family celebrations

家人

jiā rén

family members

团圆

tuán yuán

family reunion

一起

yì qǐ

together

拜年

bài nián

to visit people for New Year greetings

春节的时候，家人一起吃饭。

During Spring Festival, family members eat together.

我们去亲戚家拜年。

We visit relatives to give New Year greetings.

4. Basic festival opinions

传统

chuán tǒng

tradition

有意思

yǒu yì si

interesting

重要

zhòng yào

important

文化

wén huà

culture

我觉得中国传统节日很有意思。

I think traditional Chinese festivals are very interesting.

这些节日对中国文化很重要。

These festivals are important for Chinese culture.

Final Check

Complete

春节是中国最重要的节日。很多家人会一起庆祝，还会放烟花。我觉得中国传统节日很有意思，也很重要。

Spring Festival is the most important festival in China. Many families celebrate together and also set off fireworks. I think traditional Chinese festivals are very interesting and important.
