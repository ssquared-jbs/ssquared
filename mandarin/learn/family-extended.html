Extended Family

Learn more advanced family words and how to describe people in more detail.

1. Parents and partners

母亲

mǔqīn

mother

父亲

fùqīn

father

丈夫

zhàngfu

husband

妻子

qīzi

wife

这是我的父亲和母亲。

This is my father and mother.

Quick check

2. Children and grandchildren

儿子

érzi

son

女儿

nǚ'ér

daughter

孙子

sūnzi

grandson

孙女

sūnnǚ

granddaughter

他有一个儿子和一个女儿。

He has one son and one daughter.

Check

3. People and identity

混血儿

hùnxuè'ér

mixed-race person

留学生

liúxuéshēng

international student

都

dōu

all / both

他们都是留学生。

They are all international students.

4. Language and meaning

讲

jiǎng

to speak / talk

意思

yìsi

meaning

汉字

hànzì

Chinese character

这个汉字是什么意思？

What does this Chinese character mean?

我的父亲会讲中文。

My father can speak Chinese.

Final check

Complete

这是我的父亲和母亲。他们都会讲中文。我有一个儿子和一个女儿。

This is my father and mother. They can both speak Chinese. I have one son and one daughter.
