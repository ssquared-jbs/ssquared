4. Sentences with countries

我说中文。

Wǒ shuō Zhōngwén.

I speak Chinese.

中国在亚洲。

Zhōngguó zài Yàzhōu.

China is in Asia.

英国在欧洲。

Yīngguó zài Ōuzhōu.

The UK is in Europe.

5. Location check

Lesson complete

You have finished Countries and Places.

我说中文和英文。中国在亚洲，英国在欧洲。我喜欢看世界地图。

Wǒ shuō Zhōngwén hé Yīngwén. Zhōngguó zài Yàzhōu, Yīngguó zài Ōuzhōu. Wǒ xǐhuān kàn shìjiè dìtú.

I speak Chinese and English. China is in Asia, and the UK is in Europe. I like looking at world maps.
