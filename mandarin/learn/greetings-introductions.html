Topic 01

Greetings and Introductions

Work through each step, answer the questions, then continue.

1. Basic greetings

These are the first greetings you should recognise.

你好

nǐ hǎo

Hello

您好

nín hǎo

Hello, polite

再见

zài jiàn

Goodbye

2. Quick check

3. Introducing yourself

我叫杰瑞。

Wǒ jiào Jiéruì.

I am called Jerry.

我是英国人。

Wǒ shì Yīngguó rén.

I am British.

我十五岁。

Wǒ shíwǔ suì.

I am fifteen years old.

4. Personal pronouns

Pronouns are words like I, you, he, she and we. These are essential for IGCSE Mandarin.

我

wǒ

I / me

你

nǐ

You

他

tā

He / him

她

tā

She / her

它

tā

It

我们

wǒ men

We / us

你们

nǐ men

You plural

他们

tā men

They, male or mixed group

她们

tā men

They, female group

它们

tā men

They, objects or animals

6. Asking someone’s name

Chinese Pinyin English
你叫什么名字？ nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? What are you called?
你是哪国人？ nǐ shì nǎ guó rén? What nationality are you?
你几岁？ nǐ jǐ suì? How old are you?

2. Quick check

Lesson complete

You have finished Greetings and Introductions.

你好，我叫杰瑞。我十五岁。我是英国人。很高兴认识你。

Nǐ hǎo, wǒ jiào Jiéruì. Wǒ shíwǔ suì. Wǒ shì Yīngguó rén. Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.

Hello, I am called Jerry. I am fifteen years old. I am British. Nice to meet you.
