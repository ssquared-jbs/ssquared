Teaching and Biology

Learn words about teaching, caring, biology and taking school seriously.

1. Attitude to school

认真

rèn zhēn

serious / to take something seriously

关心

guān xīn

to care

主要

zhǔ yào

major / main

我认真学习，因为老师很关心我们。

I study seriously because the teacher cares about us.

Quick Check

2. Teaching and learning

教

jiāo

to teach

孩子

hái zi

child

够

gòu

enough

老师教孩子学习汉语。

The teacher teaches children to learn Chinese.

我觉得时间不够。

I think there is not enough time.

Check

3. Biology and extra word

生物

shēng wù

biology

鸭

yā

duck

我喜欢生物，因为生物很有趣。

I like biology because biology is interesting.

Final Check

Complete

我认真学习生物，因为生物很有趣。老师教得很好，也很关心学生。

I study biology seriously because biology is interesting. The teacher teaches well and also cares about students.
