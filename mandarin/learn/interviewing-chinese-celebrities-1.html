Famous People

Learn how to talk about celebrities, public figures and different types of famous people.

1. Types of famous people

名人

míng rén

famous person / celebrity

明星

míng xīng

star / celebrity

演员

yǎn yuán

actor

歌手

gē shǒu

singer

我最喜欢的明星是一个歌手。

My favourite celebrity is a singer.

这个演员很有名。

This actor is very famous.

Quick Check

2. More public figures

运动员

yùn dòng yuán

athlete

作家

zuò jiā

writer

导演

dǎo yǎn

director

网红

wǎng hóng

internet celebrity

我想采访一个中国运动员。

I want to interview a Chinese athlete.

这个导演拍了很多电影。

This director made many films.

Check

3. Describing celebrities

有名

yǒu míng

famous

受欢迎

shòu huān yíng

popular

成功

chéng gōng

successful

努力

nǔ lì

hardworking

这位歌手很受欢迎。

This singer is very popular.

我觉得她很成功，也很努力。

I think she is very successful and hardworking.

4. Giving basic opinions

我觉得……

wǒ jué de...

I think...

我认为……

wǒ rèn wéi...

I believe...

佩服

pèi fu

to admire

影响

yǐng xiǎng

influence / impact

我很佩服这位名人。

I really admire this famous person.

她对年轻人有很大的影响。

She has a big influence on young people.

Final Check

Complete

我最喜欢的中国名人是一位歌手。她很有名，也很受欢迎。我觉得她很努力，所以我很佩服她。

My favourite Chinese celebrity is a singer. She is very famous and also very popular. I think she is hardworking, so I really admire her.
