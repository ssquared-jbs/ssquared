Places and Trips

Learn key vocabulary for travel, destinations and tourist attractions.

1. Countries and cities

中国

zhōng guó

China

英国

yīng guó

UK

北京

běi jīng

Beijing

上海

shàng hǎi

Shanghai

去年我去了中国。

Last year I went to China.

我想去北京旅行。

I want to travel to Beijing.

Quick Check

2. Tourist attractions

景点

jǐng diǎn

tourist attraction

长城

cháng chéng

the Great Wall

博物馆

bó wù guǎn

museum

公园

gōng yuán

park

我参观了很多著名的景点。

I visited many famous attractions.

我去了长城和博物馆。

I went to the Great Wall and a museum.

Check

3. Travel activities

旅行

lǚ xíng

to travel

拍照

pāi zhào

to take photos

买东西

mǎi dōng xi

to buy things

吃美食

chī měi shí

to eat delicious food

我喜欢在旅行的时候拍照。

I like taking photos when I travel.

我在中国吃了很多美食。

I ate lots of delicious food in China.

4. Travel transport

飞机

fēi jī

plane

火车

huǒ chē

train

汽车

qì chē

car

地铁

dì tiě

subway

我坐飞机去中国。

I go to China by plane.

我们坐地铁去市中心。

We take the subway to the city centre.

Final Check

Complete

去年我去了中国。我参观了很多景点，比如长城和博物馆。我喜欢拍照，也喜欢吃美食。我坐飞机去那里。

Last year I went to China. I visited many attractions such as the Great Wall and museums. I like taking photos and eating delicious food. I went there by plane.
