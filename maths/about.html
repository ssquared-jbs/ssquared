About Maths²

Maths² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE Maths.

The notes follow the structure of the Pearson Edexcel Level 1/Level 2 GCSE (9–1) in Mathematics (1MA1) specification, covering all six content areas for both Foundation and Higher tiers. Higher-only topics are tagged.

Maths² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
