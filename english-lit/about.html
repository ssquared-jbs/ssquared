About English Literature²

English Literature² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE English Literature.

The notes follow the Pearson Edexcel Level 1/Level 2 GCSE (9–1) in English Literature (1ET0): Paper 1 (Shakespeare and Post-1914 Literature) and Paper 2 (19th-century Novel and Poetry since 1789). Quotations are only taken from out-of-copyright texts; works still in copyright are summarised, so use your own copy of the text for quotations.

English Literature² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
