About A-level Computer Science²

A-level Computer Science² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE A level Computer Science.

The notes follow the AQA AS and A-level Computer Science (7517) specification, in the same topic order as the PMT revision notes: Paper 1 (sections 1–4), Paper 2 (sections 5–12) and the NEA. They are written in our own words.

A-level Computer Science² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
