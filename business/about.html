About Business²

Business² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE Business.

The notes follow the Pearson Edexcel Level 1/Level 2 GCSE (9–1) in Business (1BS0): Theme 1 (Investigating small business) and Theme 2 (Building a business).

Business² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
