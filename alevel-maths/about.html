About A-level Maths²

A-level Maths² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE A level Maths.

The notes follow the structure of the Pearson Edexcel Level 3 Advanced GCE in Mathematics (9MA0) specification: Pure Mathematics, Statistics and Mechanics. Year 12 content is also in the AS course (8MA0).

A-level Maths² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
