About A-level Biology B²

A-level Biology B² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE A level Biology B.

The notes follow the structure of the Pearson Edexcel Level 3 Advanced GCE in Biology B (9BI0) specification, covering all 10 topics. Topics 1–4 are also in the AS course (8BI0).

A-level Biology B² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
