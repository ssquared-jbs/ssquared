About A-level Economics²

A-level Economics² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE A level Economics.

The notes follow the Pearson Edexcel Level 3 Advanced GCE in Economics A (9EC0): Theme 1 (Introduction to markets and market failure), Theme 2 (The UK economy: performance and policies), Theme 3 (Business behaviour and the labour market) and Theme 4 (A global perspective).

A-level Economics² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
