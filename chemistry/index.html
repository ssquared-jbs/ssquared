Welcome

This website is designed to help students revise Chemistry in a simple, organised and interactive way. It covers the whole Edexcel International GCSE Chemistry (4CH1) course, plus the extra Edexcel GCSE (1CH0) content.

Learn

Full revision notes for every topic, with key equations, required practicals and exam tips.

Quizzes

Test yourself with multiple-choice quizzes for each topic, with explanations, or try a mixed quiz.

Flashcards

Revise key terms, tests, equations and definitions by topic with quick flip-card practice.

How the content is labelled

Paper 2 only International GCSE content examined only on Paper 2 (shown as "C" in the 4CH1 spec)
Practical A required practical you could be asked about
GCSE 1CH0 Edexcel GCSE content that is not in the International GCSE
