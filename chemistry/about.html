About Chemistry²

Chemistry² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE Chemistry.

The notes follow the structure of the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Chemistry (4CH1) specification, covering all four topics and every subtopic, including Paper 2-only content. A fifth section covers the extra content in the Pearson Edexcel GCSE Chemistry (1CH0) course.

Chemistry² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
