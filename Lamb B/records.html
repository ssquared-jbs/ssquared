The continuing record of Lamb B — its leaders, monitors, house achievements, competitions and traditions.

Two sides of the record

Lamb B's history tells us where the House has come from. These records show what the House is doing now and what generations of Lamb B pupils have achieved.

The archive is divided into two sections: House Records, documenting the people who lead the House, and House Achievements, recording the competitions, trophies and accomplishments that form part of Lamb B's continuing story.
