About Physics²

Physics² is part of Ssquared HQ. It is a free revision platform for GCSE and International GCSE Physics.

The notes follow the structure of the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics (4PH1) specification, covering all eight topics including Paper 2-only content. A final section covers the extra content in the Pearson Edexcel GCSE Physics (1PH0) course.

Physics² is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check the official specification for exactly what you need to know.
